About WildHaven

A Safe Haven for Wildlife

At WildHaven Zoo, we focus on conservation, rehabilitation, and education. We provide natural habitats that encourage native animal behavior. Our goal is to connect visitors with the beauty of nature and inspire action to protect our planet's biodiversity.

Every ticket purchased helps support our veterinary care program and our international anti-poaching wildlife projects.

Meet Our Residents

Learn about some of the amazing animal species you will encounter during your visit.

Savanna Lion

Known as the king of the jungle, our lions live in a spacious grassland area designed to match the African savanna.

Giant Panda

Mei Mei the giant panda loves eating fresh bamboo and playing on wooden structures in her mist-cooled highland home.

Scarlet Macaw

These intelligent, colorful birds live in our tropical dome aviary, flying freely among tall palms and broadleaf trees.
